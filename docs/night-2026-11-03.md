## Research (3 agents)

- **What the leading apps do after a call:**
  - Granola, tl;dv, Fathom and Otter all sell a ledger of open items across meetings.
  - They also offer chat across meetings with links to the moment.
  - Other common features: a pre-meeting brief, a follow-up email draft, and pushing actions to Jira or Linear.
  - Users hate opaque sentiment scores (Read.ai had to add an off switch), vague coaching, and made-up action items.
- **The science of speaking:**
  - Turn-taking facts (share, monologue length, questions, interruptions) have the strongest evidence.
  - Asking more questions makes people like you more (Huang 2017).
  - More than a third of feedback interventions make performance worse when they aim at the person rather than the task (Kluger and DeNisi), hence one focus per call and no grades.
- **SAM 3.1 and emotions:**
  - SAM segments and tracks; it does not read emotions.
  - Muse Spark can describe what it sees: gaze, smile, gesture.
  - EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f) bans inferring emotions in the workplace, and video calls count.
  - Your own docs say "Halo never infers emotion".
  - The safe version: opt-in, your own face only, observable signals only ("you looked away during pricing"). That needs your decision.
