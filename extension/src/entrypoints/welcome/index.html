أهلاً بك في Halo

Halo listens to your call, puts each person's points beside them on screen, and writes the meeting page when you hang up.

  • On the callPoints, questions and decisions beside each speaker, and a coach on the side.
  • After the callA meeting page with the summary, actions by urgency and the full transcript.
  • English and ArabicHalo follows the language people speak, and the whole app switches with one tap.

The tour walks through every feature. Replay it any time with Induction, top right of the meeting pages.
